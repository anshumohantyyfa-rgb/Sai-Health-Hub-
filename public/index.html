Password-encrypted backup
Encrypts the exported backup in this browser using a password and Web Crypto (AES-GCM with a derived key). Keep the password separately; it cannot be recovered.

    BMI Guide
    

    Body Mass Index compares weight with height. For adults, these are the standard categories used by health services.
    

    

    

    How to read your number
    

      

    • BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. It does not measure body fat, muscle or fitness on its own.
      • 

    • Muscular people can score high. Athletes, pregnant and breastfeeding people should read BMI with professional guidance.
      • 

    • Track the trend, not one reading. Monthly check-ins show direction far better than a single value.
      • 

    • Pair it with daily habits. Sleep, water, movement and balanced meals matter more than the number itself.
      •
